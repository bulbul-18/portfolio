About Bulbul

I am a final-year Computer Science student and software developer. My work usually starts with a problem I have run into myself. I get curious about how it could be solved, research the options, and build the solution until it works.

I have taken that approach into two internships, in workflow automation and JSON configuration, and into competitions: All India Rank 56 at the National Entrepreneurship Challenge 2025 (Advanced track, IIT Bombay), among the top 15 teams at the Western Entrepreneurship Challenge 2025, and second place at ARDETHON. I served as webmaster of ABES ACM-W Chapter and led the team to be the first chapter featured in the ACM-W global newsletter spotlight.

Alongside building, I have helped host ACPC, the ICPC-style inter-college programming contest of our ACM chapters, as a team member in 2023 and as head in 2025.
