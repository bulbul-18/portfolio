YieldX, landing page of the live app

  1. 01

    The challenge

    Farmers decide what to plant and what to spend under weather uncertainty, pest and disease risk and price swings, often without data and rarely in tools built for their own language. YieldX explores how decision support could reach them in English, Hindi and Punjabi.

  2. 02

    What we built

    A farmer signs in and enters a crop, either by photographing it, so the app identifies the crop and flags visible disease, or by typing. Location and average rainfall are fetched automatically. The dashboard shows a yield estimate, a profitability view and a sustainability score, with charts for yield against fertilizer, yield against rainfall and profit across crops, plus a suggested next crop. The main recommendation can be read aloud, and a Kisan AI chatbot answers farming questions.

  3. 03

    My role

    Team lead for our Smart India Hackathon team. I built the frontend: the interactive dashboard with yield, profitability and sustainability views, multilingual support and the audio recommendations. I also collaborated on crop diagnosis, the chatbot and user authentication.

  4. 04

    Under the hood

    Next.js (App Router) and TypeScript, styled with Tailwind CSS and shadcn/ui. Genkit with Google's Gemini models power crop diagnosis, recommendations and text-to-speech. Charts use Recharts, forms use React Hook Form with Zod validation, data is stored in MongoDB Atlas, and sign-in uses JWT.

  5. 05

    Status and outcome

    Top 50 of 500+ teams in the college round of Smart India Hackathon 2025.

    A working hackathon prototype: the full flow runs from sign-up to dashboard, but the yield figure is illustrative, and the news and farmer quotes on the landing page are sample content.
